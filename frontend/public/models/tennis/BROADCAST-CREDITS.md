# Tactical court replay

Athlete mesh, clothing and textures from MakeHuman, fitted to the Quaternius skeleton and equipped with the project's tennis racket. See [character credits](./CHARACTER-LICENSE.md).

Body movements by Pulgarin-Giraldo et al. (2017), adapted onto the athlete skeleton. The motion data and its adaptations retain CC BY-SA 3.0. See [motion attribution and modifications](./mocap/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Netball Court by Dimitrios Savva (photography) and Jarod Guest (processing), distributed by Poly Haven under CC0. The local files are a tone-mapped 4096 × 2048 WebP backdrop and a 256 × 128 linear HDR lighting map derived from the 4K Radiance panorama. [Original panorama](https://polyhaven.com/a/netball_court) · [License](https://polyhaven.com/license)

Racket orientation comes from measured 3DTennisDS groundstrokes and volleys and the University of Bath serve dataset. The used tennis motion capture dataset was obtained from https://tennisdb.cs.pollub.pl at the Lublin University of Technology. See [trial credits, licensing and modifications](./mocap/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Court choreography, camera blocking, landing targets and ball trajectories are synthesized from the coach's board.
