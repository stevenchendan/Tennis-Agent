# Tactical court replay

Athlete mesh by Quaternius, adapted with team kit. See [character credits](./CHARACTER-LICENSE.md).

Recorded tennis strokes by Pulgarin-Giraldo et al. (2017), adapted onto the athlete skeleton. The motion data and its adaptations retain CC BY-SA 3.0. See [motion attribution and modifications](./mocap/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Stadium 01 by Sergej Majboroda, distributed by Poly Haven under CC0. The local files are a tone-mapped 2048 × 1024 WebP backdrop and a 256 × 128 linear HDR lighting map derived from the 2K Radiance panorama. [Original panorama](https://polyhaven.com/a/stadium_01) · [License](https://polyhaven.com/license)

Court choreography, camera blocking, landing targets and ball trajectories are synthesized from the coach's board.
